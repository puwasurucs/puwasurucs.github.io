Statement automation

Statement automation is a private tool used only by its owner. Once a day it finds the owner's emailed account statements and bills in the owner's Gmail, removes the password from the attached PDF, and saves it under a consistent name in a folder in the owner's own Google Drive. It then adds a label to the email so the same email is not processed twice.

The tool is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.
